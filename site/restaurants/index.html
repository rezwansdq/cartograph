    How the score works

    1. A rating with few reviews is pulled toward the city average (a Bayesian average with 50 "virtual" average reviews), so a 5.0 with three reviews can't beat a 4.5 with 900.

    2. Only stars above 3.0 count, so a cheap but mediocre place can't win on price alone. What's left is divided by the square root of the price level ($ = 1 to $$$$ = 4). For Canadian cities, price comes from what reviewers paid per person, with every $15 counting as one level.

    3. Places are ranked within their city: a score of 90 means better value than 90% of restaurants there.
